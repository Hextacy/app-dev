# My Favorite Series

My favorite series is **Stranger Things**. It's a thrilling sci-fi show with an amazing cast and nostalgic 80s vibes.

## Main Characters:
- **Eleven** - A young girl with psychokinetic abilities.
- **Mike Wheeler** - One of Eleven's close friends.
- **Dustin Henderson** - The science nerd with a heart of gold.
- **Lucas Sinclair** - A level-headed friend with a strong sense of loyalty.

## Why I Love It:
- It's a perfect blend of mystery, adventure, and nostalgia.
- The characters feel real, and the plot keeps you on the edge of your seat.

## Fun Fact:
- Did you know that **Stranger Things** was inspired by the works of Stephen King, Steven Spielberg, and John Carpenter?
